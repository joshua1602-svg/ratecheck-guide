How to challenge a business rates valuation

If you think your business property's rateable value is wrong, the Valuation Office has a formal process. This is not the same as asking your council to correct your business rates bill.

1. Check the published valuation

First, use GOV.UK to find your property's rateable value and available valuation information. Confirm its size, description, location and any details relevant to the valuation. You can compare it with genuinely comparable nearby properties.

2. Raise a Check case

For England and Wales, you generally start with a Check through your business rates valuation account. Confirm or correct the official property facts and wait for the Check outcome before starting a valuation Challenge.

3. Submit a Challenge with evidence

If you still disagree after the Check stage, a Challenge needs a valid ground, a proposed rateable value, relevant evidence and a supporting statement. Depending on the case, evidence might include reliable comparable valuations, rental details, property measurements, photographs or relevant decisions.

Deadlines vary by case and circumstances. Consult the current Valuation Office challenge guidance rather than relying on an informal estimate of your filing window.

4. Appeal if necessary

If you disagree with the outcome of a Challenge, or the relevant response deadline has passed, you may be able to appeal to the Valuation Tribunal. There are separate rules and time limits. An initial RateCheck estimate does not itself prove a valid case.
